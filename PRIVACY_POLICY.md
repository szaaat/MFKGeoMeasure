# Privacy Policy for MFK GeoMeasure

**Effective Date:** September 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **MFK GeoMeasure** ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with our mobile application available on iOS and Android.

## 1. Overview and Core Commitment
Your privacy is extremely important to us. **MFK GeoMeasure** is designed as a professional geodetic survey, GIS data collection, and educational calculation application. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data or location history.

## 2. Information We Access and Process

### A. Location Data (GPS & GNSS)
- **Usage:** The app accesses your device's GPS and GNSS positioning sensors to determine your current location, display coordinates in EOV (Hungarian National Grid) and WGS84 formats, calculate orthometric heights, and log survey points, lines, or polygons.
- **Storage:** All recorded coordinates and survey data are stored locally on your device within your local app database. Location data is not transmitted to external tracking servers.

### B. Camera and Photo Library
- **Usage:** The app requests access to your camera and photo library solely to allow you to capture and attach georeferenced photos to your local survey features and field logs.
- **Storage:** Photos and EXIF metadata remain stored locally on your device.

### C. Motion Sensors (Accelerometer)
- **Usage:** The app accesses motion sensors to operate the digital bubble level (libella) during static measurement procedures.
- **Storage:** Sensor data is processed in real time on the device and is not saved or transmitted.

## 3. Third-Party Services
The app uses standard mapping tile providers (such as OpenStreetMap, Google Maps, and public tile servers) to render background map tiles. When requesting map tiles, standard network requests are sent to these providers over HTTPS/HTTP. No personal identifying information is attached to these tile requests.

## 4. User Accounts and Analytics
- **No Account Required:** MFK GeoMeasure does not require user registration, login, or personal profile creation.
- **No Tracking:** We do not track you across third-party websites or applications, nor do we use third-party advertising SDKs.

## 5. Data Control and Deletion
All survey data, offline maps, and photo logs created within MFK GeoMeasure are stored on your device. You can clear offline maps, delete projects, or uninstall the app at any time to permanently remove all stored data from your device.
